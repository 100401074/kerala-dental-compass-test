FAQ · Kochi

Common questions about dental care in Kochi.

Which area of Kochi should I look at first?

Depends on what you need. Edappally and Kakkanad have the deepest specialist supply — oral surgery, periodontology, orthodontics. Marine Drive and MG Road favor cosmetic and aesthetic dentistry. Vyttila and Kacheripady are central and well-connected by metro. If you're flying in from the Gulf, clinics near Edappally are closest to Cochin International Airport.

Is Kochi cheaper than other Indian cities for dental treatment?

For implants and aligners, Kochi prices are typically 15–25% lower than Mumbai or Delhi for comparable quality. Within Kerala, Kochi is mid-priced — cheaper than Trivandrum medical-college-area clinics, slightly more expensive than Thrissur or Palakkad.

I'm an NRI. Can I do consultations remotely?

Several Kochi clinics offer pre-arrival WhatsApp consultations to assess if your case can be treated during a short visit. Message us with your travel window and treatment needs — we'll match you to clinics willing to start with a remote consultation.

How do I know a Kochi clinic's pricing upfront?

Most clinics will quote on WhatsApp once you describe the treatment plan. The cost guides we publish are indicative ranges across Kochi — useful for budgeting, not exact quotes. We do not accept clinic-supplied pricing without verification.

What if I have a dental emergency?

United Smiles Family Dentistry offers emergency services with same-day appointments. Message us on WhatsApp with "EMERGENCY" in the first line and we will route your request to a clinic with same-day capacity.
