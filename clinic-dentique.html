The bottom line

Best for anxious patients and complex restorative work.

The strongest pattern in this clinic's reviews — turning up in 40% of the ones we read — is Dr. Sampreeth Mathew's gentle approach making procedures like root canals "smooth and almost pain-free". If you have dental anxiety, if you have been putting off complex restorative work, or if you need careful orthodontic or implant work, this is a clinic worth considering.

The clinic is also mentioned by international patients in our review data, including from Maldives and the UK, which suggests they handle the practical side of cross-border care reasonably well.
