Dental implant cost
in Kerala, 2026.

What a single implant actually costs in Kochi, Calicut, Trivandrum and Thrissur — pulled from real partner-clinic pricing and verified review data, not marketing claims.

AM

Reviewed by Dr. Aleena Mathew, BDS, MDS Orthodontics

Practicing orthodontist · Kerala Dental Council registered · Editorial review of all surgical guides on this site.
Last edited 27 May 2026 · Updates monthly.

If you are asking how much a dental implant costs in Kerala, the honest answer is that it depends — on the implant brand, on whether you need a bone graft, on the city, and on whether your clinic charges separately for the crown that finishes the implant. This guide gives you real price ranges from Kerala clinics across all four major cities so you can plan a realistic budget before your first consultation.

The headline number

A standard single dental implant in Kerala in 2026 costs between ₹28,000 and ₹65,000, including the implant, the abutment, and the ceramic crown. This is the range across mainstream tier-A and tier-B clinics. Premium clinics using top-tier implant systems may charge up to ₹85,000–₹1,10,000. Below ₹28,000 is unusual and we recommend asking specifically which implant brand is being used.

An orthodontist's note

Patients sometimes see a "₹15,000 implant" advertised and assume it covers the full procedure. In nearly every case, that price covers only the titanium screw — the abutment, the crown, and any scans are charged separately. Ask for an all-inclusive quote before committing.

City-by-city price comparison

Within Kerala, prices vary by city — both because of real differences in clinic operating costs and because of supply density. Kochi and Trivandrum tend to be at the higher end. Thrissur and Palakkad are more affordable for comparable quality.

What actually drives the price

1. The implant brand

The single biggest cost driver. Implant systems made by Straumann (Switzerland), Nobel Biocare (Switzerland/USA), and Astra Tech (Sweden) are the premium options at ₹35,000–₹50,000 for the screw alone. Mid-tier systems from Osstem (Korea), MIS (Israel) or Dentium (Korea) typically come in at ₹20,000–₹30,000 with comparable clinical evidence. Lower-tier systems exist below this but with less long-term evidence and weaker warranty terms. Ask which brand is being used, and verify the warranty period.

2. Whether bone grafting is needed

If you have lost a tooth several years ago and the surrounding bone has resorbed, your jawbone may not be dense enough for an implant without grafting. A CBCT scan at your first consultation will reveal this. Grafting adds ₹8,000–₹35,000 depending on technique, and may add 3–6 months to your treatment timeline because the graft needs to integrate first.

3. The crown that finishes the implant

The crown sits on top of the implant and is what you can see and chew with. Ceramic crowns are the standard at ₹6,000–₹10,000. Zirconia crowns are more durable and aesthetic at ₹10,000–₹14,000. Avoid quotes where the crown is unspecified.

4. The dentist's experience

An implant is a small surgical procedure with a measurable failure rate. Dentists with specific implantology training and 100+ implants placed have demonstrably better success rates. This justifies a premium of ₹5,000–₹15,000 over a generalist. On clinic listings we surface the specific dentist who patients credit with implant treatments in their reviews.

The hidden costs people forget

  • CBCT scan (₹1,500–₹3,500). Required before placement. Sometimes bundled, sometimes billed separately.
  • Antibiotics and pain medication (₹400–₹1,200). Mostly a 5-7 day prescription after placement.
  • Multiple visits. An implant procedure spans 3-6 months from start to crown. Plan for 4-6 visits.
  • Follow-up checks (free at most clinics). But confirm.
  • Travel + accommodation if you are an NRI. Time your visits so the major sittings line up with your Kerala stay.

Multiple implants — the rule of thumb

If you need more than one implant in the same visit, most Kerala clinics will discount the second and subsequent implants by ₹3,000–₹8,000 each — because the surgical preparation, scans, and bed time are amortised.

NRI patients — how to plan a Kerala implant trip

About a third of the implant inquiries we receive come from Malayalee NRIs in the UAE, Oman, Saudi and Qatar — usually patients who want the procedure done in Kerala while visiting family. A few practical points:

  • The procedure spans 3-6 months. Most NRIs structure it as two trips — one for placement, one for crown fitting — spaced 3-4 months apart.
  • WhatsApp consultation before flying lets the clinic confirm whether your case is suitable, what brand they would use, and what the all-in quote is.
  • Kochi clinics near Edappally are closest to Cochin International Airport — useful if your visit is short.
  • Get the CBCT scan in your first visit so the surgery date is committed before you return home.

Frequently asked questions

Are Kerala implants of comparable quality to those done abroad?

Yes — assuming you choose a clinic using a premium or mid-tier implant brand (Straumann, Nobel, Osstem, MIS, Dentium) and a dentist with implant experience. The procedure and the materials are the same. What differs is cost, not clinical outcome. Several of our reviewed Kochi clinics treat patients from the UK and Maldives precisely for this reason.

What happens if the implant fails?

Implant failure rates in 2026 are about 2-5% over 10 years. Most clinics offer warranty replacement of the implant itself if it fails for an integration reason within a specified window (typically 5-10 years). Confirm the warranty terms upfront and get them in writing.

Is bone grafting always required?

No — it depends on how much bone you have. Patients who have had a missing tooth for less than 6-12 months usually have enough bone for direct placement. Patients who have been missing teeth for many years may need grafting first. A CBCT scan reveals this.

Can I do it in one visit?

Conventional implants — no, because the screw needs 3-6 months to integrate with bone. Immediate-loading implants are sometimes possible when bone density is favourable, but most Kerala dentists will only recommend this if your specific case is suitable. Ask whether you are a candidate.

What's the cheapest legitimate option?

Around ₹26,000–₹32,000 all-in, using a mid-tier brand like Osstem or MIS, at a Thrissur or Palakkad clinic. Below ₹24,000 we would ask specifically what corners are being cut — usually the crown material or the brand of implant.
