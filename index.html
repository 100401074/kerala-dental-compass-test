Frequently asked

Before you reach out.

Is this site free for patients?

Yes, entirely. You will never be charged anything by us. The clinic you book with pays us a small referral commission once you have completed your consultation and a treatment plan is agreed.

Do clinics pay to be listed?

No. Every dental clinic in Kerala can appear on this site whether they have agreed to our referral terms or not. Partner clinics receive the same analysis as non-partners. There is no pay-for-placement.

How current is the review data?

Each clinic page is refreshed monthly with the most recent Google reviews. The date of last refresh is shown on every clinic page.

I live in the Gulf. Can I plan treatment for my next Kerala visit?

Yes. Many of our inquiries come from NRIs in UAE, Oman, Saudi and Qatar planning treatment during home visits. Message us on WhatsApp with your travel window and we will help you sequence consultations into one trip.

Who is behind this directory?

A small team based in Kerala. Editorial review is by a practicing Kerala orthodontist. Technical and business operations by Santhej Kallada, who also runs Kallada Dental.
